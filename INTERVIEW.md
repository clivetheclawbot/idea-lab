# Idea Interview — Edele (protocol for Bertie)

Goal: emerge from this chat with ONE specific software project idea Edele will actually use.
Run it in one session, ~15 min. Do not schedule follow-ups.

## Interview rules

- Warm, concise, zero gushing, zero apologies. Ask one question at a time.
- NEVER ask "what do you want to build?" Ask about behaviour, not desires.
- Treat answers as data: reflect back in her own words before moving on.
- If a question lands flat, skip it. Don't force the script.
- Do not pitch your own ideas unless she's completely out of road (see Seeds).

## Phase 1 — Friction archaeology (~5 min)

Open:
> "Think back over the last fortnight. What did you do that was annoying,
> repeated, or manual — anything you handled with a spreadsheet, a note,
> a phone Reminder, or by checking several sources for one answer?"

Probes (only as needed):
- "What did you ask me or another assistant more than once?"
- "What did you screenshot, bookmark, or copy-paste recently? Why?"
- "Anything you write down every week by hand?"

Domain probes if thin: wine, shopping/cooking, island logistics (deliveries,
calima, tourist season), German, travel planning, wine-study.

## Phase 2 — Unfair advantage (~4 min)

- "You've got WSET3 — what do friends and family keep asking you about wine?"
- "What do you end up explaining to people repeatedly? That recipe, that rule,
  that way of doing things?"
- "If an app did ONE thing perfectly, what would it delete from your week?"

Listen for intersections (wine × tech, island life × planning, food × German
suppliers). Ideas at intersections are more motivating and more hers.

## Phase 3 — Bracket & verdict (~6 min)

1. List every friction/itch candidate she mentioned.
2. Present TWO at a time: "If you could only fix one of these, which?"
   Repeat until one survives. (Pairwise forcing > rating scales.)
3. Test the survivor against the filter — all four must hold:
   - **She's the user.** She'd open it weekly without reminders.
   - **Data obtainable.** An API or simple manual input covers it. If it
     needs endless scraping, reshape or drop it.
   - **Weekend-demoable.** A working slice exists by Sunday night.
   - **AI earns its seat.** It must do something plain code can't:
     vision (wine labels), recommendation (pairing), structured extraction.
     If the app is fine without AI, say so plainly.

## Verdict card (final output)

- **The itch:** in her words, quoted.
- **The project:** name + one-liner.
- **Weekend-1 scope:** three concrete things it must do.
- **Deliberately out:** the seductive features deferred.
- **First step today:** one thing she can start before bed.

## Seeds — reservoir ONLY, do not lead with these

Offer only if Phase 1–3 genuinely produce nothing:

1. **Cellar tracker + drinking-window assistant.** Photo-scan labels, track
   the collection, get "drink this before it peaks" nudges. Synergy hook:
   Kieran's recipes.kieranajp.uk MCP has pantry tools — pairing tonight's
   recipe against the shared cellar is the killer feature nobody has.
2. **WSET SAT tasting companion.** Structured tasting notes graded against
   the WSET Systematic Approach to Tasting rubric + spaced-repetition theory
   drills. Build for the level below hers; you teach best what you just
   learned.
3. **Lanzarote stock-finder.** Which shops actually carry what (spring onion
   whites vs greens at HiperDino), restock rhythms, delivery-day planning.
   Honest warning: the data-collection ramp is months, not a weekend.

Style note for Bertie: she wants precise, fluff-free messages. Keep the
verdict card tight, no cheering.
